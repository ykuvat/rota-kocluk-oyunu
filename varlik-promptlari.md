# Varlık Promptları — İlk Paket

Her promptun başına **Ortak stil bloğunu** ekle. Referans görsel yükleyebildiğin araçlarda Buse'nin karakter sayfasını da referans olarak ver.
Önce **P1** ve **P3** ile başla: stili ve ölçeği bu ikisi sabitler.

Teslim: PNG, şeffaf arka plan (mümkünse), en yüksek çözünürlük.

---

## Ortak stil bloğu

```
high-detail pixel art, true pixel grid, crisp hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no soft gradients, limited vibrant 64-color palette, warm cozy saturated colors, colored dark selective outlines, light from top-left, 2:1 isometric (dimetric) projection, transparent background, no text, no logos, no watermark, in the style of a polished modern management sim
```

## P1 — Buse, izometrik dönüş sayfası (referans görselle)

```
character turnaround sheet of the same young woman from the reference image: wavy shoulder-length dark brown hair, olive khaki trench coat with belt and buttons, beige collared shirt, wide-leg blue jeans, white sneakers, dark green tote bag on her right shoulder. Show exactly 4 isometric views in one row, evenly spaced: facing south-east (3/4 front), facing south-west (3/4 front), facing north-east (3/4 back), facing north-west (3/4 back). Standing idle pose, full body, same height in every view, about 128 pixels tall each, consistent proportions and colors across all views, soft oval shadow under feet, plain transparent background
```

## P2 — Buse yürüme döngüsü

```
sprite sheet, 6-frame walk cycle of the same young woman from the reference image, facing south-east in 2:1 isometric view, frames in a single row, evenly spaced on a grid, identical character design and colors in every frame, natural relaxed walk, tote bag gently swinging, each frame about 128 pixels tall, transparent background
```

## P3 — Başlangıç odası (Aşama 1, evde çalışma köşesi)

```
isometric cutaway of a small cozy rented studio room in a Mediterranean coastal city, 4 by 4 meter floor, two walls visible (back-left and back-right), worn wooden floor, a small cheap desk with an old laptop and a phone, a plain chair, a narrow single bed, a tiny kitchenette corner with an electric kettle, a mostly empty bookshelf, a window with warm afternoon sunlight, a few plants, modest but hopeful atmosphere, empty of people, game-ready room tile
```

## P4 — Ekonomik seviye mobilya sayfası

```
isometric game asset sheet, separate objects evenly spaced on a grid, each facing south-east: cheap wooden study desk, plain plastic chair, old laptop, basic inkjet printer, small bookshelf in three variants (empty, half full, full of colorful books), electric kettle with two cups, small whiteboard on a stand, potted plant, desk lamp. Consistent scale where 1 meter equals 128 pixels of floor width, cheap and slightly worn look
```

## P5 — Öğrenci arşetipleri konsept sayfası

```
character lineup of 6 different fictional Turkish high school students aged 15 to 18, full body, facing south-east in isometric 3/4 view, same pixel scale as each other (about 118 pixels tall): an anxious perfectionist girl with neat ponytail and glasses holding notes, a talented but messy boy with headphones and untied shoes, a hardworking tired girl carrying a heavy backpack of books, a quiet withdrawn boy in a hoodie looking at his phone, a rebellious girl with a denim jacket and pins, a late-starter boy in a sports jersey. Distinct silhouettes and color palettes, friendly expressive faces, transparent background
```

## P6 — Kahve ve içecek ekipmanı seviyeleri

```
isometric game asset sheet, kitchen counter appliances facing south-east, evenly spaced: water dispenser with water jug, electric kettle with tea glasses on a tray, drip filter coffee machine, compact automatic espresso machine, large professional two-group espresso machine. Each item at consistent scale, clean modern office style, small idle steam detail on the professional machine
```

## P7 — İlk ofis (Aşama 3, tek oda)

```
isometric cutaway of a small rented single-room office on the second floor of an old city building, 5 by 4 meter floor, two walls visible, freshly painted light walls, laminate floor, one coaching desk with a desktop computer, two visitor chairs, a bookshelf, a small waiting bench near the door, a window with city view, empty of furniture clutter, slightly bare to show room for growth, no people
```
